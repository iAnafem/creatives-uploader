Creative Uploader

A scheduling tool for musicians to publish their own videos to Instagram, TikTok and YouTube and to see the results in one place.

What it does

Creative Uploader lets a band or artist connect the social accounts they own, upload a finished video, write a caption for each platform, and choose a publishing time. At that time the application posts the video on the user's behalf. Afterwards it collects the public performance metrics of those posts, such as views, likes and comments, and shows them together.

Who it is for

It is built by and for the metalcore band Underfault and used by its members to manage the band's channels. Access is by invitation.

Platforms

The application uses the official Instagram Graph API, the TikTok Content Posting API and the YouTube Data and Analytics APIs. It never asks for account passwords. Each connection is authorised through the platform's own login screen and can be revoked at any time.
