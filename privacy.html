Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

Creative Uploader ("the application", "we") is a tool that publishes videos to social media accounts on behalf of their owners and reports how those videos perform. This policy explains what data the application handles and why.

1. Data we collect

Account information. When you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address and profile picture to identify you inside the application.

Platform connections. When you connect an Instagram, TikTok or YouTube account, the platform gives us an access token and a refresh token limited to the permissions you approve. We store these tokens encrypted and use them only to publish the content you schedule and to read the statistics of that content.

Content you upload. Videos, captions, titles and scheduling details that you add to the application are stored until you delete them.

Performance metrics. We retrieve public and owner-level statistics of the posts the application published, such as views, reach, likes, comments, shares, saves and watch time, and store them to show you trends over time.

We do not collect data about other people's accounts, and we do not use tracking cookies or advertising identifiers.

2. How we use the data

Only to provide the service: authenticating you, publishing content you scheduled to accounts you connected, and displaying analytics for that content. We do not sell data, share it with advertisers, or use it to train machine-learning models.

3. Google API Services

The application's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. YouTube data is accessed through the YouTube API Services; by using the application you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service and acknowledge the Google Privacy Policy. You can revoke the application's access to your Google account at any time at https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions.

4. Meta and TikTok

Instagram data is accessed through the Instagram Graph API under the Meta Platform Terms. TikTok data is accessed through the TikTok Content Posting API under the TikTok Developer Terms. You can revoke access in your Instagram or TikTok account settings.

5. Storage and security

Data is stored in a database controlled by the application operator. Platform tokens are encrypted at rest. Access to the database is limited to the operator. The application does not transfer data to third parties other than the platforms named above, and only to perform the actions you requested.

6. Retention and deletion

Disconnecting a platform account deletes its tokens immediately. Deleting a scheduled or published post in the application deletes its stored video and metrics. You can request deletion of your whole account and all associated data by contacting us; we complete such requests within 30 days.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with an updated date.

8. Contact
